# VitalLens — Complete Audit
**Date:** 2026-09-05 · **Scope:** technical, product, business, economic, legal, strategic
**Method:** direct code reading, four parallel deep-dive audits, live database queries, live exploit verification against the running server, dependency scanning, git history. Every critical finding below was independently re-verified. Assumptions flagged **[UNVERIFIED]**.

## 1. Executive summary — worst first

### 1. The app invents health data, presents it as clinical findings, and ships it in the production bundle. [CRITICAL — SAFETY]

`src/utils/stool-analyzer.js` opens with the literal comment `// Simulated stool analysis engine` and contains **13 `Math.random()` calls**. It produces, and the UI renders as measurements:

- **Transit Time** — `12 + Math.random()*40` hours. Pure random.
- **Hydration %** — `Math.random()*40 + 55`. Pure random.
- **Microbiome Diversity %** — `Math.random()*30 + 55`. Pure random. **The app has no microbiome data of any kind.**
- **Bowel frequency** — drawn at random from four strings including "1x every 2 days (low)".
- **Gut health score** — randomised jitter, and it feeds the dashboard's global Health Score via `health-score.js:50`.

Worse than the fabricated numbers is the fabricated *advice*. The colour verdict is derived from crude averaging of ~100 sampled pixels — non-deterministic, so the same photo yields different results — and it emits strings including:

> *"Upper GI bleed possible — seek medical attention"*
> *"Bile duct obstruction possible — urgent medical review"*
> *"Lower GI issue possible — consult physician"*
> *"Possible fat malabsorption or celiac indicators"*

These are rendered at `stool-scanner.js:176` under the heading **"Color Analysis"**, persisted to the user's permanent record at `:104`, and **the page carries no disclaimer of any kind.** I confirmed this code is present in the built artifact `dist/assets/index-B9fegyo1.js`.

This is dangerous in both directions. A false urgent flag causes panic and unnecessary care-seeking. A false *"Normal — healthy bile processing"* on a genuinely black stool could **delay care for a real gastrointestinal bleed.** That is a foreseeable-harm scenario, and it is the single largest liability in this codebase — larger than every security hole combined.

It is not isolated:
- `src/utils/apple-health.js:13` — `return true; // Force mock mode for browser testing`. Because `window.HealthKit` never exists in a browser, **every step count is `Math.random()*9000 + 3000`**, rendered as "Steps Today" with a goal ring and history chart.
- `src/services/foodScanApi.js:319` — when the barcode API fails, it returns a **fabricated product** with `healthScore: 50, rating: 'Good'`, which is then written to the user's scan history and shown as a real analysis.
- `src/pages/dashboard.js:199` — a **hardcoded seven-point trend** `[72,74,71,76,78,75,78]` renders under "Weekly Trend → Health Score" for any user with no history.
- `src/utils/health-score.js` — a brand-new account with zero data receives a composite **66, Grade "C"**, displayed in a 160px hero ring. There is no "insufficient data" path, and 28% of the score's weight can never change because the dashboard never passes those inputs.
- `src/pages/body-scanner.js:1103` renders **"Suggested Lab Tests — these tests would help confirm or rule out the visual findings above"**, plus a Scoliosis Screen, risk tiers, and triage chips reading `seek_attention` — attached to a heart rate that `biomarker-engine.js` will manufacture from a photo of a wall.

Your own consent gate promises the app *"is not a medical device and does not diagnose or assess any condition."* The live code contradicts that promise in at least five modules.

### 2. Live production secrets sit in plaintext on disk. [CRITICAL]
`server/.env.test` is not a template. It holds the real `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` (bypasses every RLS policy on the live project), plus live OpenAI, Anthropic, Stripe, Upstash and VAPID keys and plaintext account passwords. **Verified good news:** `git log -S` across both repos confirms none ever entered git history. They remain exposed on disk and in every copy of this directory. Rotate today.

### 3. Two holes in the one control your entire data isolation depends on. [CRITICAL]
Only 1 of 34 route files uses the RLS-scoped client; the rest use the service-role key that bypasses RLS. The global ownership guard is therefore *the only thing* separating users' health records — and it has two confirmed bypasses:
- **Unauthenticated billing IDOR — verified live.** `GET /api/billing/status?userId=<any-uuid>` returned **HTTP 200** with subscription data and no `Authorization` header, because billing mounts at `server.js:107`, three lines above `requireAuth` at `:110`.
- **Multipart bypass — working proof-of-concept.** `express.json` skips `multipart/form-data`, so `req.body` is `{}` when the guard reads it; multer populates `userId` afterwards. An attacker can pass a victim's id to `/vision-scan`, `/biomarker-scan`, `/parse-labs` — zeroing a paying user's quota and mis-attributing spend.

### 4. After five months and 22,132 lines, the product has never had a user. [CRITICAL]
Three accounts exist: two test fixtures and you. Zero paying subscribers. Seven AI calls ever, $0.065 lifetime spend. There is no product-market signal of any kind.

### 5. Nobody can sign up, and nobody can pay. [CRITICAL]
The Supabase "Confirm signup" template renders with no confirmation link, so real users can never activate — found 2026-07-09, still open, a two-minute fix that has blocked all acquisition for two months. Separately the frontend never calls the billing endpoint, no price appears on any screen, and `upgradeRequired` is never handled. Revenue is structurally impossible.

### 6. Two headline AI features do not work, and one bills you to fail. [CRITICAL]
`server/routes/ingest.js:18` — the endpoint called after *every* write — logs a line and returns `{queued:true}` without ever calling `ingestEvent`. **The RAG corpus is essentially empty**, yet every chat message still pays OpenAI for an embedding to search it. And `health-copilot.js:258-289` calls the app's own API without forwarding the auth header, so four of eight copilot tools 401 every time while the agent loop re-sends the full conversation up to five times — one "run my patterns" message costs ~5× a normal one and still fails.

### 7. The spend caps do not hold. [CRITICAL]
Four independent failures: `spend-guard.js:26` sums rows with no aggregate and **PostgREST caps responses at 1,000 rows**, so past 1,000 monthly calls the $250 global ceiling can never be reached; three AI routes never call the guard at all; the guard **fails open** on any DB error — including the weekly Supabase auto-pause; and `usage-gates.js` uses a non-atomic read-modify-write with no unique constraint, so one race permanently disables the gate for that user.

**What is genuinely good:** RLS on every table, versioned append-only consent, a correct Mifflin-St Jeor implementation (verified to the digit), a regulatory-language CI lint, XSS escaping where applied, and a spend-control architecture that is right in concept. Zero `var`, clean ES modules. `services/prompts.js` deduplication is exactly the right pattern. The hygiene-scanner and product-results copy ("Worth Reviewing", "Something to Explore") is a model of correct framing — it should be the template for the whole app.

#### Code quality & tests

- **One test file exists in the entire repo** (17 runtime cases). Frontend: **0%** of pages, utils, services tested; no test script or runner in the root `package.json`. Server: 12 of 80 endpoints touched (**15%**), **0** with any behavior test. Billing, vision, copilot, and every spend-cap decision are untested.
- **That one suite never runs in CI. [Critical]** `server/.github/workflows/ci.yml` gates it on `env.SUPABASE_URL != ''` with no workflow- or job-level `env:` block, so the condition is always false. The root workflow runs no tests at all.
- **`health-input.js:12` sends a hardcoded `'default-user'`. [Critical]** The `vitallens_user_id` key it reads is **never written anywhere** (verified: zero writes). All eight resulting requests 403; because a 403 doesn't throw and the reads have no `else`, the **Substances, Background, and Goals tabs render permanently empty with no error.** Analytics consequently shows "0 Supplements" forever.
- `main.js` reads `session?.userId`, which doesn't exist (`session.user.id` does) → **the entire notification system, ~200 lines, never runs** while a floating widget tells the user "Notifications: Enabled".
- `getSession()` destructures unguarded and throws on network failure, aborting boot → blank page. `isOnboardingComplete()` returns `true` on error, so a transient failure makes a new user **skip onboarding entirely**.
- **Five competing ways to get the current user id**, two permanently broken.
- 3 files exceed 800 lines and hold 53% of page code; longest function 376 lines.
- **Duplication:** Supabase client constructed **36×** across 32 files (twice inside request handlers); `dotenv.config()` **39×**; two parallel DB layers; the same 500-handler **65×**; UTC date math **47×** — so "today" flips at UTC midnight regardless of the user's timezone.
- **~800 LOC dead**, incl. 46 unused exports and two React components imported but never mounted (one shadowed by a vanilla reimplementation of itself). `correlate.js:28` contains a committed heredoc terminator `ENDOFFILE` as a bare statement — it parses, so CI's `node --check` misses it.
- **1,494 inline `style=` attributes**, 960 in four files, bypassing a 2,215-line design system.

#### Data

- `user_id` is **uuid in ~7 tables, text in 9**, so those nine **cannot have foreign keys or `ON DELETE CASCADE`**. [High]
- **Account deletion is therefore incomplete — a GDPR Art. 17 exposure.** It relies on a hand-maintained list that omits at least `api_cost_log`, `usage_tracking`, `user_consents`, `cycle_log`, `medication_log`, `water_log`, `hygiene_scans`, `genomics_traits`, `practitioner_links`. [High]
- **No baseline schema. [High]** The 11 committed migrations alter tables whose `CREATE TABLE` exists nowhere, and there's no `supabase/config.toml`, so they cannot be replayed. With no PITR on the free tier, **the schema exists in exactly one place — the live project.**
- Indexes on the hot paths (`daily_nutrition(user_id,date)`, `meals(user_id,logged_at)`, `usage_tracking(user_id,feature,window_start)`) are absent from the repo and unverifiable.

#### Performance & reliability

- `requireAuth` **builds a new Supabase client and makes a remote call on every request** — 50–200 ms per route, with no `.catch()`, so a non-auth rejection hangs the request forever.
- The context cache is **never invalidated** (`invalidateContextCache` has zero callers) and has no single-flight lock — N concurrent cold requests fire **15N** queries.
- `ai-fetch.js` retries 3× with no jitter atop 30 s timeouts → **127 s worst case**; `src/utils/api.js` sets no client timeout at all.
- **Sentry captures nothing. [Critical]** The custom handler at `server.js:167` responds without calling `next(err)`; `setupExpressErrorHandler` sits at `:175` and is never reached. 27 of 36 routes never call `next(err)` either. **One line moved fixes it.**
- **The service worker lies about saved data. [High]** `sw.js:99` returns a synthetic `200 {success:true}` for any failed POST — the UI confirms a meal was logged when it wasn't. The replay queue stores the original ~1-hour token, so replays 401 forever and grow without bound.
- No graceful shutdown; `/api/health` returns ok unconditionally without touching Supabase, so the platform healthcheck stays green while the database is paused.
- Dashboard streaks are structurally capped at 5/5/**1** days; `getWeeklyScores` orders ascending with `limit(7)`, returning the **oldest** seven scores forever.

#### DevOps

- **Correction worth stating precisely:** the backend *is* version-controlled and backed up — `server/` is its own repo with remote `vitallens-server`, 0 unpushed commits. It's invisible to the *root* repo, which is why a naive check suggests otherwise. The real gap is the **frontend: 22k lines with no git remote, existing only on this Mac**, and its CI can never run. [Critical]
- Two repos, no cross-repo gate; `multer@1.4.5-lts.2` is end-of-life; no staging environment.

### 2.2 Product & UX

**~39 distinct features across 16 pages** — roughly 5–10× viable scope for one person. **Twelve backend features have zero frontend callers**: cycle, water, medications, early-patterns, custom-correlation, correlation-engine, user-data export/delete, oura, usage, push, billing. Cycle tracking and medications, described as features, **have no UI at all**.

**GDPR gap. [Critical]** `/api/user-data/export` and `/delete` are fully implemented server-side but have **no UI entry point anywhere** — the only account action is Sign Out. Users cannot export or delete their health data.

**Onboarding: 12–14 screens to first value**, including **forced TOTP MFA enrolment whose "Skip for now" doesn't persist — so it re-prompts on every single login, forever.** The notification permission prompt fires on app boot before the user has done anything. No back button exists on any onboarding step. The consent gate offers a "Health-Data & AI Processing" document that **does not exist**, and `isConsentComplete()` **fails open**, letting users into a health app with no consent recorded.

**Failure is rendered as "you have no data."** Every page catches load errors and falls through to its empty state — "No lab results logged yet", "0 Check-ins" — so a network failure is indistinguishable from an empty history. `profile.js:30` renders a **blank profile form** on a failed load, and the next save overwrites the real row with empties: a data-loss path. Two body-scanner paths leave the UI spinning forever on an unhandled rejection. `navigator.onLine` appears **zero times** in the codebase.

**Accessibility — the weakest area, and legally relevant (ADA/§508).**
- **Zero `aria-*` attributes. Zero `role=` attributes. Zero `<label for=>` associations against 77 `<label>` elements** — every text field in the app is announced unlabeled.
- **The bottom navigation is unreachable by keyboard** — built from `<div>`s with click handlers only, on every screen. The dashboard's 12 primary controls are all `onclick` divs.
- `base.css:122` sets `outline: none` globally with only two rules restoring it — **no visible focus indicator** on nearly every control (WCAG 2.4.7 failure).
- **Pinch-zoom is disabled** (`index.html:5`, `user-scalable=no`) — a WCAG 1.4.4 failure and unacceptable in an app of small numbers.
- **Contrast failures throughout** (computed): `--text-tertiary #9A9A9A` = **2.48:1** and carries real content everywhere; error text renders at **2.30:1** — the messages a blocked user *must* read are the least readable in the app; `--accent-coral`, `--accent-red` and `--accent-amber` all alias to the same colour, so danger and warning are visually identical. The **legal disclaimers are the least legible text on the page** at 9–10px tertiary.
- Charts have no `role="img"` or text alternative; the only way to read exact values is a mouse-only tooltip — on a mobile-first app.
- **There is no web app manifest** — despite being described as a PWA, it fails installability outright.
- 14 control types fall below the 44px touch minimum, including destructive buttons at ~23px.

### 2.5 Legal, compliance & risk

**Fabricated medical output is now the dominant legal exposure. [Critical]** §1.1 is not a wellness-framing problem that better copy solves. Randomised output reading *"Upper GI bleed possible — seek medical attention"*, persisted to a health record with no disclaimer, is a product-liability and consumer-protection (FTC §5 deceptive practices) exposure independent of FDA. The strongest defence — that you are a general-wellness journal — is directly contradicted by your own shipped code.

**FDA / medical-device exposure. [High]** Photographing eyes, skin, or stool and returning an assessment sits near the §201(h) boundary; intended use is inferred from the product's totality, not disclaimer text. The language purge and CI lint were right, but **the hardening is not airtight**: the dead root `biomarker.js` still carries `pallor_present` / `drooping_present` / `fungal_pattern`, and the lint's ban list omits those terms, so CI passes. Body-scanner's "Suggested Lab Tests… confirm or rule out" and Scoliosis Screen are clinical acts in live code.

**Terms of Service — good draft, three blockers. [High]** Genuinely above average (non-device framing, AI-limitations clause, liability cap, no-training commitment). Blockers: `[JURISDICTION]` unset, arbitration unfinished, `support@vitallens.app` apparently doesn't exist **[UNVERIFIED]**.

**Privacy policy — strong, one factual error. [Medium]** Correctly names processors, GDPR bases, retention, transfers, and even cites Washington's MHMDA. **Error:** it describes Upstash as the "background-job queue," but that queue was deleted. A policy that misdescribes processing is a compliance defect.

**Erasure obligations unmet. [High]** Per §2.1, deletion orphans data in 8+ tables — and per §2.2 there is no UI to request it at all.

**Missing artifacts. [Medium]** No DPAs evidenced (GDPR Art. 28 requires them before any EU user); no RoPA; CPRA sensitive-data right-to-limit unimplemented. **Recommend geofencing to US-only at launch.**

**HIPAA — likely inapplicable, one trapdoor:** practitioner sharing, if a covered-entity clinician uses it for treatment. Freezing was right; deleting is better.

**Age gate is client-side only** and the DOB isn't persisted, so no attestation evidence is retained. Cycle tracking carries post-*Dobbs* exposure for a feature that has no UI anyway.

## 4. Overall viability verdict

**Pivot — narrow drastically, and remove the fabrication modules before anything else touches a user.**

The engineering instincts here are real. Row-level security on every table, versioned consent, a spend-guard architecture, a regulatory CI lint, and a correct metabolic model are things most funded seed teams skip, and they are why this verdict is not "kill." But the audit found that nearly every one of those defenses has a hole: the guard is bypassable two ways, the spend cap silently stops working past a thousand calls, the error monitor captures nothing, the only test suite never runs, and the RAG corpus the AI searches is empty. One pattern explains all of it — **things were built but never verified end-to-end** — and it is the same root cause as the empty eval folder, the untested signup email, and the unwired checkout.

The fabricated-data finding is different in kind, and it dominates. Shipping `Math.random()` under the label "Microbiome Diversity", and shipping the string *"Upper GI bleed possible — seek medical attention"* generated from pixel noise into a health record with no disclaimer, is not a bug to schedule. It is the one thing in this codebase that could hurt somebody and end the project. It must be deleted today, before any other work, and independently of whether you pivot, continue, or pause.

Strategically, five months and 22,000 lines have produced thirteen feature areas and zero units of user validation. The constraint was never engineering capability — it was that building became a substitute for shipping. Every added feature expanded the regulatory surface and the maintenance burden while adding no validated value, and the three things that would let you learn something — a working signup email, a price, ten real users — remain undone because they are less enjoyable than building the fourteenth feature.

The path forward is unglamorous and specific. Delete the fabrication modules today. Spend one day on items 4–17, most of which are one-line fixes with disproportionate consequences. Then strip the product to food scanning plus wellness targets, measure whether the scanner is actually accurate, and put it in front of ten real people within two weeks. If D7 retention is non-trivial, rebuild outward from evidence. If it isn't, you will have learned that for the price of two weeks instead of another five months. **The riskiest thing you could do right now is write another feature.**
