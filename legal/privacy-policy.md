# VitalLens Privacy Policy

**Version:** 2026-07-08
**Status:** DRAFT — REQUIRES REVIEW BY A LICENSED HEALTHTECH ATTORNEY BEFORE LAUNCH.

VitalLens ("we", "us") is a personal wellness journaling application. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have. We take an intentionally minimal, consent-first approach because the information you log is sensitive.

## 1. Who we are and scope
VitalLens is a general-wellness product. **It is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose, treat, cure, prevent, screen for, or assess any disease or health condition.** Nothing in the app is medical advice. Always consult a licensed healthcare provider for medical concerns.

## 2. What we collect
- **Account data:** email address, authentication identifiers.
- **Profile data you enter:** age, height, weight, biological sex, wellness goals, and any conditions you *choose* to self-report. Self-reported conditions are context you give us; we never infer them.
- **Wellness logs:** meals and nutrition, water, supplements, sleep, exercise, mood/energy check-ins, menstrual-cycle entries (if you use that feature), medications you choose to log (for your own records only — we never provide dosing or interaction guidance), and free-text notes.
- **Photos you submit:** food photos, and — if you use the optional Body Check-In — facial or body photos. Photos are sent to our AI processors for analysis and are **not** retained by us after processing unless you explicitly save a result. We do not use your photos to train any model.
- **Device/technical data:** app version, basic diagnostics, and error reports.
- **Integrations you connect (optional):** wearable data (e.g., Oura) that you authorize.

We do **not** knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

## 3. Biometric and health-data notice
Facial photographs and heart-rate estimates derived from video may constitute **biometric** or **consumer health data** under laws such as the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA), the Washington My Health My Data Act (MHMDA), and similar state laws. We process this data **only** with your explicit, separate consent, **only** to produce the wellness reflection you requested, and we **do not** sell it, share it for cross-context advertising, or retain the source imagery after processing. You can withdraw this consent at any time in Settings.

## 4. How we use your data
- To provide the core service: log your data, compute wellness scores, and surface patterns/correlations across your own logs.
- To operate AI features you invoke (food analysis, wellness reflections, the copilot chat, weekly summaries).
- To maintain security, prevent abuse, and debug errors.

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** use it for third-party advertising.

## 5. AI subprocessors and third parties
To provide AI and data features we transmit the minimum necessary data to:
- **OpenAI** — food-image analysis and embeddings.
- **Anthropic (Claude)** — wellness reflections, copilot chat, summaries.
- **Google Fonts** — font delivery (loads on page view).
- **Supabase** — database, authentication, storage (our primary processor).
- **Upstash** — background-job queue.
- **Sentry** — error monitoring.
- **Stripe** — subscription billing (we never see your full card number).
- **USDA FoodData Central & Open Food Facts** — nutrition lookups (queried by food name/barcode, not tied to your identity).
- **PostHog** (if enabled) — privacy-respecting product analytics.

Each processor acts under its own terms and a data-processing agreement. We send only what a given feature requires.

## 6. Legal bases (GDPR/UK GDPR, where applicable)
Where EU/UK law applies, our bases are: **consent** (health/biometric special-category data, Art. 9(2)(a)); **contract** (providing the service you signed up for); and **legitimate interests** (security, service improvement) balanced against your rights.

## 7. Data retention
We keep your account and logs until you delete them or close your account. Submitted photos are deleted after processing unless you save the result. Cost/usage and security logs are retained for up to 24 months. On account deletion we remove your personal data within 30 days, excluding narrow records we must keep for legal/financial compliance.

## 8. Your rights
Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, WA MHMDA, and others) you may:
- **Access / export** your data — available in-app (Settings → Export My Data).
- **Delete** your data and account — available in-app (Settings → Delete My Account).
- **Correct** inaccurate data.
- **Withdraw consent** (including biometric/health-data consent) at any time.
- **Opt out** of any "sale"/"sharing" — we do not sell or share, so there is nothing to opt out of, but you may still submit a request.
- **Non-discrimination** for exercising these rights.

To exercise rights not available in-app, email **privacy@vitallens.app**. We respond within the timeframe your law requires (typically 30–45 days).

## 9. Security
We use encryption in transit and at rest, row-level database security that isolates each user's data, scoped API access, and least-privilege service credentials. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify you and regulators of a qualifying breach as required by law.

## 10. International transfers
Data may be processed in the United States and other countries where our processors operate. Where required, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards.

## 11. Changes
We will post the new version here and update the date. Material changes affecting how we use sensitive data will require your renewed consent.
